Here's the presentation from my ICCV 2025 <a href="https://2coool.net/">keynote</a> in Hawaii. Thanks to organizing team of the 2nd Workshop on the Challenge Of Out Of Label Hazards In Autonomous Driving. The presentation sheds light on a crucial, yet understudied area. While we've made great strides in object detection and geo-localization of driving scene objects, conflation of these objects, including unexpected road closures, to digital maps at scale & the involved autonomous driving navigation methods remain less studied.
